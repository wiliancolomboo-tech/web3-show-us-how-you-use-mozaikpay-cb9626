Here is how we use MozaikPay to scale Solana commerce 👇🧵

2/4 ⚡ The Problem:
Traditional checkout drops off when merchants need to coordinate multi-party payments (affiliates, platform fees, treasury reserves) and match on-chain slots with tax invoices.

Solution:
MozaikFlow combines MozaikPay's seamless checkout with custom on-chain routing! 💸

3/4 🛠 How It Works:
1️⃣ MozaikPay triggers checkout & on-chain settlement
2️⃣ mozaik_settler Anchor contract executes zero-downtime multi-party splits (Vendor/Platform/Affiliate)
3️⃣ Node.js worker verifies HMAC-SHA256 signatures & RPC finality
4️⃣ Instant Resend receipts & Slack alerts
